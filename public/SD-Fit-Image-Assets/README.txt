Design note
The images are generated illustrations and sizing references, not a standardized garment-size chart.
Actual clothing measurements vary by brand, garment cut, region, and fabric. Do not infer a guaranteed
size from body shape alone; let users consult each brand's measurement chart.

Files are JPG, organized by audience. Crop dimensions are approximate panel crops from the master sheet.
